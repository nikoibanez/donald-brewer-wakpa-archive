Biographical references

Clarkson University Art Gallery maintains an artist biography with collection associations including Akta Lakota Museum, the Crazy Horse Memorial, Red Cloud Indian School, United Tribes Technical College and the Willis Tower. The original biography should be consulted for the wording of each claim. Don has additionally described a Penn State painting acquired through an alumnus's collection donation. The specific works remain to be located and independently documented.

Murals and community projects

Don's oral history references Denver-area school murals with middle-school students, murals around Globe and San Carlos in Arizona, traditional regalia and dance, and future public-art interests. No photographs or specific project dates have yet been verified for those historical murals. Those details should not be treated as independently confirmed commissions. Don has also described service in the U.S. Marine Corps and several years as a firefighter with San Carlos Apache; dates and independent records are still being sought.

How this catalogue is maintained

Artist-owned reproductions are shown with permission. Source links identify where each image or listing was found. Artwork descriptions document visible composition rather than claiming knowledge of the artist's intentions. This is a working portfolio, not a catalogue raisonné; additions and corrections are welcome.

For corrections or documentation, contact the portfolio coordinator.
