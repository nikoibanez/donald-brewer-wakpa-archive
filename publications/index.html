What counts as an exhibition?

Press, museum artist files, historical advertisements, card reproductions and auction listings demonstrate professional circulation of artwork. They do not count as exhibitions merely by being published. For a grant requiring a recent exhibition history, document the dates, venue, title, curatorial or selection details, and at least one invitation, program, catalog or venue confirmation.
