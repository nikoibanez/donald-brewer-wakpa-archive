Collections and archives

Clarkson University Art Gallery lists Don’s work in the Akta Lakota Museum, Red Cloud Indian School, Crazy Horse Museum, United Tribes Technical College Collection and Chicago’s former Sears Tower (now Willis Tower). The particular paintings, accession numbers and current display status have not been verified from each institution.

The Heard Museum artist archive also confirms an artist file in the Billie Jane Baguley Library and Archives and a record in the Native American Artists Collection (RC62). This refers to archival materials, not proof of ownership of a painting in the museum’s object collection.

For grant applications: These records document professional exhibitions from 1988–1992 and recorded market appearances in 2007 and 2010. They do not establish ten qualifying exhibitions during the past ten years. Recent exhibitions, if any, still need to be documented separately.
