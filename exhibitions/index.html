Collections and archives

Clarkson University Art Gallery lists Don’s work in the Akta Lakota Museum, Red Cloud Indian School, Crazy Horse Museum, United Tribes Technical College Collection and Chicago’s former Sears Tower (now Willis Tower).

The Heard Museum artist archive also holds an artist file in the Billie Jane Baguley Library and Archives and a record in the Native American Artists Collection (RC62).
