01 / THE ARTIST

Between ancestral memory and contemporary color.

Donald Brewer, who paints under the name Wakpa, identifies as an enrolled member of the Cheyenne River Sioux Tribe. Raised in Thornton, Colorado, he describes formative summers with family at Cheyenne River and Pine Ridge. His painting combines Dakota and Lakota cultural subjects with modernist and impressionistic approaches.

A veteran of the U.S. Marine Corps, Brewer has produced decades of painting and is named in university and museum collection references. In a recent interview, he described prior mural projects with schoolchildren, building and dancing in traditional regalia, and developing an increasingly abstract oil-painting practice. Project details without outside documentation are labeled as artist-reported.
