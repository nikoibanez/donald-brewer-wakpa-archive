About Don

Donald Brewer, who paints under the name Wakpa, is an artist with Cheyenne River Sioux roots. Raised in Thornton, Colorado, he spent summers with family in South Dakota. His paintings return to the landscape of the Plains, the movement of horses and dancers, and the presence of people within vast open skies.

For years Don worked primarily in acrylic, developing layered color and precise transitions between figures, clouds and land. More recently, he has begun a new period of experimentation with oil paint and increasingly abstract compositions. He describes the switch in medium as a chance to learn all over again.

Don also makes traditional regalia and participates in powwow dancing. His experience in community art includes work with students and murals, alongside an extensive painting practice.

Exhibitions and awards

Public Art

Painting beyond the studio.

School murals

Don recalls working with Denver-area middle-school students and teachers on collaborative murals for grades six through eight. The schools and project dates are being researched.

Murals in Arizona

He also recalls murals on buildings around Globe and San Carlos, Arizona, including a Geronimo-themed work that no longer survives. Archival photographs are being sought.

New work in oils

After decades of acrylic painting, Don is exploring a more abstract direction in oils while continuing his figurative work. New pieces will be added as they are documented.
